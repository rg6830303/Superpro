Decisions worth flagging

Real photographs, graded to the palette. Both plates are the images supplied, not renders. They are pushed through a soft-light teal grade with a volt bloom rather than shown raw, because a daylight snapshot dropped straight in front of the ball and the mark reads as a different piece of film. Each one drifts and pushes in, so it never sits still. No captions on either, as asked.
Licensing is still worth checking. These look like third-party photographs rather than your own. Confirm you hold a licence before this goes in front of the public.
No wordmark anywhere. The sequence ends on superpro-mark alone, per the brief — the lockup with the word never appears.
No player. Only a paddle and a ball. Without a body to carry the swing, the paddle arrives fast, rotates through the ball and leaves frame.
